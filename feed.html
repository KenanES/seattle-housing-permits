Data methodology and accounting notes

Key statistical definitions and SDCI dataset nuances to keep in mind when citing figures.

Mean versus median turnaround times

Permit review durations are heavily right-skewed: simple over-the-counter permits clear in days while stalled or appealed multifamily towers can take years. Both median and mean (plus 75th and 90th percentiles) are reported side by side. Negative date artifacts (13 of 72,332 records) are excluded.

Site-level multi-permit unit accounting

SDCI frequently attaches a development site's proposed housingunitsadded to paired demolition (-DM), shoring, and phased construction (-PH) permits. Active review totals reflect raw SDCI permit rows; the major developments ledger defaults to building permits only to reduce double counting.

2026 year-to-date completion bias

Construction duration (issued to completed) for permits issued in 2026 (104.8d mean) only reflects fast-finishing projects already completed within the same calendar year. Use 2016 to 2023 cohorts for unbiased construction duration benchmarks.

SoQL date type handling

Building Permits (76t5-zqzr) stores dates as floating timestamps, whereas Land Use Permits (ht3q-kdvx) and Trade Permits (c87v-5hwh) store YYYY-MM-DD strings requiring explicit ::floating_timestamp casts in live SoQL queries.
