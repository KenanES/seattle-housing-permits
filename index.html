Seattle housing permits fell 59% below their decade norm, even as review times sped up.

Through November 2026, the city issued 4,442 new housing units against a ten-year average of 10,814 per year. Meanwhile, median building permit approval eased to 58.5 days and Land Use Master Use Permit issuance improved 22% faster than the decade norm. SDCI open data verified Nov 25, 2026 (records through Nov 23, 2026).
